# Tempo

Tempo keeps a focus timer, today's tasks, and music in one window.

[Download Tempo for Windows, macOS, or Linux](https://github.com/ahmelkholy/tempo/releases/latest). Extract the archive and open **Tempo**.

- **Timer** — Choose **Start focus**, **Short break**, or **Long break**. Change their lengths in **Settings → Timer**. Choose **Settings → Appearance → Interface size** to scale text and controls together. Tempo remembers your zoom and window size.
- **Tasks** — Add a task and check it off when you're done. Unfinished tasks carry over to the next day. Google Tasks sync is optional under **Settings → Tasks**.
- **Music** — Choose a song, playlist, or YouTube Mix under **Music → Change…**. Tempo remembers your selection. Press **Play** to listen. By default, breaks pause the track and resume it where you left off. Connect Chrome for your personal Mix order.

For Python setup and music requirements, see the [installation guide](docs/USER_GUIDE.md#run-from-source).
