The desktop archives include yt-dlp 2026.11.23 and Deno 2.9.8. The build verifies the release files against their SHA-256 digests. The Python installation uses the yt-dlp package in its environment. mpv is installed separately.

- yt-dlp publishes its code, release binaries and license information, including notices for bundled dependencies. [yt-dlp 2026.11.23](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp/tree/2026.11.23#license)
- Deno publishes its runtime under the MIT license. [Deno license and third-party notices](https://github.com/denoland/deno/blob/v2.9.8/LICENSE.md)
